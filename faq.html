Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is CalcX free?

Yes, CalcX provides free online tools for students.

2. How can I calculate percentage?

Open the Percentage Calculator, enter your marks and total marks, then click Calculate.

3. What is CGPA?

CGPA is the average of grade points earned across subjects or semesters.

4. Can I use CalcX on mobile?

Yes, CalcX is designed to work on mobile phones and computers.

5. What is the Study Planner?

It helps students organize their study tasks and schedule.

6. Do I need to create an account?

No, you can use the available tools without creating an account.
