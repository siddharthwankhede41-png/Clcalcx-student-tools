1. How to Calculate Percentage

Percentage shows how much you scored out of the total marks.

Formula: (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example: If you score 400 out of 500:

(400 ÷ 500) × 100 = 80%

2. What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your overall academic performance using grade points.

Always check your board or university rules for the correct CGPA calculation method.

3. How to Calculate Age

Age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the current date.

Our Age Calculator helps you find your age in years, months and days.

4. Why is Study Planning Important?

A study plan helps students organize subjects, set goals and manage their available study time.
